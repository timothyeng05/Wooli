The Verdict

Choose Chase Sapphire if you:

  • ✓ Want maximum transfer partner flexibility with 40+ airline/hotel programs
  • ✓ Prefer a lower annual fee with strong credit value ($795 vs $895)
  • ✓ Use the flexible $300 travel credit (no category restrictions)
  • ✓ Value dining at Sapphire Reserve Exclusive Tables partners
  • ✓ Want a Visa card with excellent everyday earning (3x dining)

Choose Amex Platinum if you:

  • ✓ Stay at Hilton, Marriott, or other hotel chains (elite status perks)
  • ✓ Fly Delta frequently (Sky Club access)
  • ✓ Use premium lounges everywhere (Centurion + Priority Pass + Delta)
  • ✓ Want the highest total annual benefits ($3,500+)
  • ✓ Prefer Amex's Resy dining network (10,000+ restaurants)

Bottom Line

Chase Sapphire Reserve is best for flexible travelers who maximize transfer partners. American Express Platinum wins for frequent hotel/airline users with specific brand loyalties. Both cards offer exceptional value when their credits are fully utilized.
