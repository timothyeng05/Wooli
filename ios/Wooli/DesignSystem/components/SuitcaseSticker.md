# SuitcaseSticker

Milestones you've collected, shown like stickers on a suitcase: slightly tilted, in the colour of the group they belong to.

**Proposed:** `SuitcaseSticker(_ title:, group:, shape:)`, collected on the Today screen and in Settings → "Your stickers". A new one appears once, quietly, in the module where you earned it.

**Look:** a group-colour fill with `on-tint` text (`footnote` 700), in one of three shapes: a round oval, a pill, or a square-cornered label. Stickers are tilted −6°, +4° and −2° in turn, with a 2pt flat drop.

**Milestones:** a review streak ("All caught up 4 weeks running"), months on budget, places stamped, cities visited, films ranked.

**Consumer provides:** a short title (three words at most) and its group.

- Do: show earned stickers only. Never show locked ones or progress bars toward them.
- Don't: use stickers for notifications, nags or anything that asks you to act.
