# Institutions

Bank and brokerage logos bundled in the app for `InstitutionLogo`, matched by name: Chase, Fidelity, Schwab, American Express ("amex"), Citi, HealthEquity, Capital One, Bank of America ("bofa"). Each one is a third-party trademark, used only to identify the user's own connected account.

Show them only inside a circular clip at 40pt with a 1px `border` ring. Don't crop them differently, recolour them or use them as decoration.
